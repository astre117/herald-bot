Q: When does the class start?
A: The cohort officially kicks off September 1, but honestly it really starts for you the moment you join — there's no need to "wait" for a fixed date. The full program runs about 10 months.

Q: How long does the cohort last?
A: About 10 months from when you join.

Q: How do I get my Dravonis access?
A: Once you're confirmed as a paid student, an admin will assign your Dravonis student ID and DM it to you directly. If it's been more than 24 hours since payment, ping an admin here.

Q: What is Dravonis?
A: Dravonis is CreateHaven's AI writing tool built on the CreateHaven Writing Standard — it helps with outlining, hooks, and drafting for serialized fiction. Your student ID gives you lifetime access plus all future updates.

Q: What platforms does this cohort teach for?
A: The cohort focuses on Webnovel and PocketFM. Webnovel is where you get signed first — that's how you build your foundational expertise — before moving into PocketFM. Covers hook engineering, retention mechanics, and platform contracting logic for both.

Q: What's included in the cohort?
A: Outline and chapter templates, a constantly-updated prompt library, the CreateHaven Writing Standard, lifetime Dravonis access, weekly 1:1 classes, sprint events, and direct access to an admin.

Q: What is the refund policy?
A: There's a 4-Try Guarantee — if things don't work out, you get a refund or a guaranteed placement on an alternative platform.

Q: How do payment plans work?
A: Both flat and installment payment options are available. Talk to an admin to set up whichever works for you.

Q: How do I learn to use Dravonis?
A: There's a full walkthrough video in the Resources topic/group — check there first. If you're still stuck after watching it, use /reportissue to flag it and an admin will follow up.

Q: Where do I find the resources and templates?
A: Everything — templates, the prompt library, the Dravonis tutorial video, and resource drops — lives in the Resources group/topic. Check there first before asking.

Q: Dravonis isn't working / I found a bug, what do I do?
A: Use /reportissue followed by what's going wrong, e.g. "/reportissue Dravonis won't load my outline." It gets logged and sent straight to an admin — no need to DM separately.

Q: What happens if I miss a payment?
A: Your Dravonis access gets paused until the payment is made. Reach out to an admin as soon as you can if something's come up — it's easier to sort out before it becomes a bigger gap.

Q: What if I join mid-cohort, do I get everything from the start?
A: Yes — once you're in, you have access to everything that's been shared so far, not just what happens from your join date forward.

Q: What exactly counts as a "try" in the 4-Try Guarantee?
A: One try = you outline → it's passed through Dravonis → you do your chapter outlines → passed through Dravonis → you write your chapters → passed through Dravonis → you upload to Webnovel → and Webnovel rejects it. That's try #1. Try #2 is editing the book based on feedback and going through the process again. This repeats up to 4 tries before the guarantee (refund or alternative platform placement) kicks in.
